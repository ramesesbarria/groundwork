## Goal
One setup question sets `experience` to `new` or `experienced`, which changes explanations, the approval stop, decisions and the default approval mode (decision 0002). Planned changes: PLAN.md §10.1.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Both values are documented where they change behavior

## How to check
