## Goal
`gw-next`, `gw-approve` and `gw-reject`: the heart of the loop.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `gw-next` picks the lowest-numbered card whose dependencies are all `done`, and runs tester → implementer → reviewer
- [ ] `gw-next` stops at `awaiting-approval` in per-card mode
- [ ] `gw-approve` refuses when Evidence is empty or a linked file is missing, and otherwise commits as `[<id>] <title>`
- [ ] `gw-reject <reason>` records the reason under History and sends the card back to the implementer
