## Goal
The planner may recommend an option, clearly labeled. "Go with your recommendation" is an explicit choice, recorded as such (decision 0001). Planned changes: PLAN.md §9.2.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A recommendation is always labeled as one
- [ ] The human's acceptance is recorded in their words
- [ ] "You decide" with no reply still isn't accepted as a choice
