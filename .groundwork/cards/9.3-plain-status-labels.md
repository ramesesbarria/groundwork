## Goal
`groundwork status` and the approval messages show plain labels (to do, being built, waiting for you, done, sent back). The internal statuses don't change. Planned changes: PLAN.md §9.3.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Every `CARD_STATUSES` value maps to a label
- [x] The internal statuses in the card files are unchanged

## How to check
Run `npx groundwork-ai status` in this repo: counts read "31 done · 16 to do", and titles such as 7.5's show without quote marks.

- Tests before: 14 fail, including the existing status test once its expectations moved to the labels ([output](../evidence/9.3/failing-before.txt)); after: 428 pass, and the type check passes ([output](../evidence/9.3/test-output.txt))
