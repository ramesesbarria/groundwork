## Goal
An existing project with failing tests, lint warnings or no tests doesn't block every card forever: the rule becomes "no new failures". Planned changes: PLAN.md §9.8.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The baseline is saved and linked from HANDOFF
- [ ] The roles judge by "no new failures" when a baseline exists, and by "all pass" otherwise
- [ ] The no-tests case leads to a decision, not a dead end
