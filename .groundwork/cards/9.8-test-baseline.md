## Goal
An existing project with failing tests, lint warnings or no tests doesn't block every card forever: the rule becomes "no new failures". Planned changes: PLAN.md §9.8.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The baseline is saved and linked from HANDOFF
- [x] The roles judge by "no new failures" when a baseline exists, and by "all pass" otherwise
- [x] The no-tests case leads to a decision, not a dead end

## How to check
Read step 4 of `core/guides/existing-project.md` (the baseline), then "Checks" in `core/workflow.md` (all pass, or no new failures with a baseline).
