---
title: "Decisions"
description: "Record a choice and its reason once, and agents stop reopening it."
---

**A new session can read your code. It can't read why.** A decision record keeps the options you
weighed and the reason for the pick. Agents are told not to reopen one without you.

## Record one

```bash
npx groundwork-ai decide "Use cookie sessions, not JWTs" --status accepted
```

This creates the next numbered file in `.groundwork/decisions/` and updates the index. `--status` is
`proposed` (the default), `accepted` or `superseded`. Then fill it in:

```markdown
---
id: 0001
title: Use cookie sessions, not JWTs
status: accepted
date: 2026-11-15
---
## Context
What needs deciding, and why now.

## Options
2 to 4 options, each with pros, cons and the cost of switching later.

## Decision
The option the human chose, in their words if possible.

## Consequences
What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what to revisit.
```

Record what the human chose, never the agent's own preference. If you took its suggestion, the record
says "accepted the recommendation".

## The index

`.groundwork/DECISIONS.md` is generated. Don't edit it: change the record, then run
`npx groundwork-ai index`. Every session's brief lists the decisions in force.

## Replace one

Write a new record and set the old one to `superseded`. `compact` then moves the old one to
`.groundwork/archive/decisions/`. Nothing is deleted.

## With the flow on

With the [flow](/docs/flow) on, `gw-decide <topic>` walks you through it, and the planner runs it
before any task depends on a choice. Groundwork never picks your stack:

1. It writes 2 to 4 options, each with trade-offs.
2. It waits for your choice.
3. It records your answer in your words.

The agent may add one line starting **Recommendation:**. It is always labeled, and it is never the
decision. With `experience: new` you get one by default; otherwise only if you ask. Saying "you
decide" makes the agent ask once whether to go with its recommendation, and only a clear yes counts.
